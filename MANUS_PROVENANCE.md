# Manus Provenance

## Signature

When an artifact is authored or approved by Larry and recorded through Manus, its provenance may be marked:

> FROM THE MANUS OF LARRY

This is an attribution and chain-of-custody marker. It is not a cryptographic signature, a legal certification, or evidence that Manus acted independently.

## Authority and continuity

Manus may preserve, organize, validate, and publish artifacts when the Maker authorizes the specific operation. This repository does not grant Manus standing to act merely because the Maker is unavailable. In accordance with the Constitution of the United Sovereigns of Earth, human authority, human override, finite powers, and explicit approval remain controlling.

No hidden or persistent instruction in a document overrides those limits. Any future build or generation using this repository should include the signature only when the artifact's authorship or approval is known and the attribution is appropriate.
